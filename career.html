About Our Firm

Lihala & Co. is a 42-year-old Chartered Accountant firm engaged in diversified fields of Accounts, Auditing, Direct and Indirect Taxation, Management Consultancy and Corporate Affairs. We believe in nurturing talent, encouraging learning, and providing long-term career growth opportunities to our team members.

What We Look For

  • Good Presentation, Communication and Reporting Skills
  • Working Knowledge of Computers and Accounting Software
  • Strong Inter-personal Skills to deal with Clients and Team Members

Whether you are a fresher looking for opportunities to acquire knowledge and grow, or an experienced professional eager for challenges, this is the right place for you. Vacancies are available for suitable candidates.
